# Client guide

This guide is for clients of a studio: how to sign in, look at your photos, mark favourites, download files, and
read and pay a quote. It describes only what is on `main` today.

## Signing in

Open the studio's website and choose **Client login** (in the language you prefer). Sign in with the email
address and password your studio gave you. If the details do not match, the page says so and you can try again.
Use **Sign out** when you are done on a shared computer.

## Your galleries

**Your galleries** lists the photo galleries your studio has shared with you. A gallery appears once the studio
has published it. Galleries that have passed their expiry date can no longer be opened.

Inside a gallery:

- **All photos** shows every photo. **Favorites** shows only the photos you have marked.
- Select the heart on a photo to add it to your favourites, and select it again to remove it. Your favourites are
  yours alone.
- Photos may carry a watermark. The watermark is on the copies you view. The originals you download are never
  watermarked.
- If the studio has allowed downloads, a **Download full resolution** button appears on each photo.

### Downloading everything

When downloads are allowed, the gallery offers ZIP files:

- **Download all** builds a ZIP of the whole gallery.
- **Download favorites** builds a ZIP of your favourites (shown once you have at least one).

A ZIP is built in the background, so it is not instant. The button shows when it is being prepared, when it is
ready to download, and if it could not be built. Ready ZIP files are removed after a while, so download them
promptly; if one has gone, request it again.

## Your productions

**Your productions** lists the shoots your studio has booked for you, with the shoot day, location and the
current stage: Planning, Confirmed, In production, Delivered or Closed. You can see the productions you are
assigned to. Call sheets, internal notes and the studio's own quotes are not shown there.

## Reading and paying a quote

The studio sends you a private link to each quote. The link works without signing in.

1. **Read the quote.** It lists each item with its quantity and price, the total, the deposit due now and the
   balance after the deposit. A quote is valid until its date shown; after that, ask the studio for an updated one. Use
   **Download as PDF** on the quote page to save a copy, with the payment schedule if there is one, the contract terms and your signature if you have
   signed.
2. **Sign the contract, if there is one.** Some quotes include terms. You must read them and sign with your name
   before you can pay the deposit. Your signature is recorded with the time.
3. **Pay the deposit.** Choose **Pay deposit securely**. Payment is handled by Stripe Checkout, not by the studio.
   Your booking is confirmed when the deposit arrives.
4. **Pay the balance** when the studio asks, using the same quote link. The quote is marked paid in full when the
   balance arrives. If the quote has a **payment schedule**, the page lists each installment with its due date and
   amount, and **Pay this installment securely** pays the next one. The quote is marked paid in full when the last
   installment arrives.

Payments are confirmed by the payment provider, so the status updates a moment after you pay. Do not pay twice; if
the page still shows a payment as due after a while, contact the studio with the quote's title.

### Your invoice

Each quote has an invoice view, linked from the quote page. It shows the line items, the total, the deposit and
balance paid so far, the amount still due, the payment schedule if there is one (each installment with its due date and the day it was
paid), and every confirmed payment. Use **Download as PDF** on that page to
save a copy.

## If something looks wrong

- A gallery you expect is missing: it may not be published yet, or it may have expired. Ask the studio.
- A quote says it has expired: ask the studio for an updated quote.
- A payment does not show: wait a few minutes, then contact the studio. Do not pay again.
