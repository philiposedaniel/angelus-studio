# Events: guide for clients, guests and vendors

What people outside the studio see and do for an event. Studios can share this page, or parts of it,
with their clients. Staff work is in the [staff manual](events-staff-manual.md).

## If you are hosting the event (organizer)

The studio can name you as an organizer of your event. You then manage its guest list yourself.

1. Sign in to the client portal with the account the studio created for you. Your portal home
   shows **Your events**.
2. Open your event to see the guest list: who is invited, how many seats each invitation has,
   and who has answered, with the names of the people coming and their meals. When the event
   offers meal choices, the page also shows how many chose each meal, ready for the caterer.
3. **Add a guest** with their name, and optionally email, phone, group, party size and the
   language their invitation opens in. **Change** or **remove** guests the same way.
4. **Copy invitation link** next to each guest gives you their personal invitation to send by
   email, WhatsApp or text. Each guest's link is theirs alone: send it only to them.
5. **Download the guest list** as a spreadsheet at any time.
6. If the studio sends email, you get an email each time a guest replies or changes their reply,
   with who is coming and their meals. Ask the studio if you would rather not get them.

The studio handles the invitation design, seating, vendors and the event details. Ask them for
changes to those.

## If you are invited (guest)

1. Open the personal link you were sent, by email from the studio or by message from the host.
   It shows the invitation, the programme and the map.
2. Say whether you are coming and how many of your party are coming, give the names of the
   people you bring, choose a meal for each person if the hosts offer a choice, and add any
   dietary needs or a note for the hosts. You can change your answer until replies close.
3. Keep the link: it shows your **entry code**. Show it at the entrance, on your phone or printed.
   If the studio sends email and you gave an email address, you may get a reminder to reply, and
   a reminder with your link the day before the event.
4. If the event has a live stream or a photo album, the invitation links to them.

## If you are buying a ticket

1. Open the event page from the studio's website and choose a ticket and the number of places.
2. Fill in your name, email and (optionally) phone. If you have a **promo code**, type it in the
   promo code box; the discount is applied before you pay.
3. A free ticket opens straight away. A paid ticket takes you to a secure card payment page; your
   places are held for you while you pay.
4. Your ticket page shows the event, the places it admits and your **entry code**. Save the link to
   that page: it is your ticket. Anyone with the code can use it, so keep it to yourself. If the
   studio sends email, the link also arrives in your inbox once the ticket is confirmed, and again
   in a reminder the day before the event.
5. **Refunds** are made by the studio. Contact them; once refunded, the ticket stops working.

### When an event is sold out

1. Join the **waitlist** on the event page with your name, email and the places you want. You get
   a private waitlist link showing your place in line.
2. If places free up, they are offered to people in the order they joined. When it is your turn,
   your waitlist link says the places are held for you, and until when (24 hours).
3. Press **Take my places** (or **Pay and take my places** for a paid ticket) before then. If you
   do not, the places go to the next person.
4. You can **leave the waitlist** from the same link at any time.

If the studio sends email, you also get an email when places are offered. Check your waitlist link
either way.

## If you are a supplier (vendor)

1. Open the private link the studio sent you for the booking.
2. It shows the event, your role, when to arrive (in the event's local time) and the studio's
   instructions.
3. **Confirm** the booking, with a note for the studio if needed.

The link stops working if the booking is cancelled. Payments and contracts are arranged with the
studio directly.

## Privacy

- Every link (invitation, ticket, waitlist, vendor) is personal and unguessable. Share it only
  with the person it is for.
- Hosts never see the studio's internal notes about guests. Vendors never see amounts.
- Pages behind these links are not shown to search engines.
